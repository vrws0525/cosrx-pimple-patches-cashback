## What a hydrocolloid patch actually does (and doesn't)

None of these are medicated. They're hydrocolloid dressings, the same class of material used on wounds, and the mechanism is mechanical rather than chemical: the patch seals the spot, draws out fluid and sebum, and keeps the area moist while blocking bacteria and your fingers.

That's the whole trick, and it explains the results people report. On a whitehead or a pimple that has already come to a head, it can pull the contents out overnight and flatten the spot. On a deep, blind or cystic pimple that hasn't surfaced, there's nothing to draw out, and the patch does little beyond protecting it. Reviewers say this in almost exactly those words: great on whiteheads, "not blind pimples."

Also worth knowing before you buy: the patch is not waterproof. A shower means replacing it.

> Hydrocolloid absorbs and protects. It doesn't dry anything out, and it won't fix hormonal or cystic acne on its own. If that's your main problem, a patch is a spot tool, not a treatment plan.

## Original Fit vs Clear Fit: the difference that actually matters

The naming makes this look like a formulation upgrade. It isn't. It's thickness.

**Original Fit** is the thicker, slightly silicone-feeling patch. Three sizes in the box, which is genuinely useful, because a 12mm patch on a 2mm whitehead is a waste of adhesive surface and a 7mm patch on a jawline cyst is useless. COSRX positions it as overnight, intensive care.

**Clear Fit** is thin and matte, all one size (10mm), and designed to sit under makeup. It's the one you can plausibly wear to a meeting.

Two details from user reviews that the marketing doesn't mention:

- The thinner Clear Fit tends to hold better through the night if you move around a lot, and the pre-cut backing sheet makes it easier to apply without folding the edges. The Original's edges crease more easily.
- The Original is thick enough that several Sephora UK reviewers said they'd never wear it out of the house, and a few called it poor value per patch once they'd tried other brands.

So the honest split: Original Fit for overnight and for matching patch size to spot size, Clear Fit for daytime and for sleeping on your side.

## How to apply one so it survives the night

Most complaints about COSRX patches come down to application, not formula.

1. **Cleanse, then dry completely.** Oil, toner or serum under the patch means it won't seal, and an unsealed patch does nothing.
2. **Apply before your skincare, not after.** This is the single most common mistake. Essence or moisturiser first is why patches slide off.
3. **Pick a patch wider than the spot.** It needs to grip healthy skin on all sides.
4. **Don't touch the adhesive side.** Handle the edges only.
5. **Press flat.** Air bubbles and creases become leak paths.
6. **Leave it 6 to 8 hours, up to about 12.** Replace it once it turns white and opaque, which means it has absorbed fluid.
7. **Don't reuse it, and replace it after showering.**

One caveat from a reviewer worth repeating: don't layer a patch over an active like retinol. Someone did exactly that and reported skin damage in the area. Patches go on bare, clean skin.

## Quick answers

**Do they work on blind or cystic pimples?** Largely no. Both COSRX and independent reviewers say the patches work on spots with visible fluid. On deep inflammation there's nothing to absorb, though the barrier still stops you picking at it.

**Can I wear one under makeup?** Clear Fit, yes, with realistic expectations. It's thin and matte, but reviewers still describe it as visible under certain lighting. Matte foundation over the top helps. Don't expect invisible.

**Can I shower with one on?** No. The patches aren't waterproof, so plan around your shower rather than through it.

**Is the 96-patch set worth it?** On cost per patch, yes, by a wide margin. On shelf life, check the expiry date on the pack you receive before committing to a multi-year supply.
